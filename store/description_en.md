# Mariy Kechyshot — app description (EN)

## Title
Mariy Kechyshot — Mari calendar

## Short description (≤80 chars)
Mari calendar: holidays, prayers, folk omens, rites and birthdays

## Full description
Mariy Kechyshot ("Mari Calendar") is a calendar of traditional Mari holidays, community prayers and folk omens, in Meadow Mari and Russian.

Inside the app:
• Today — holidays, prayers, omens and birthdays of notable Mari people for any day. When a day has no dated omen, an "omen of the day" from folk beliefs is shown.
• Month — a calendar with colored dots, so holidays and prayer days stand out at a glance.
• Holidays — meaning and rituals (Shorykyol, Kugeche, Agavairem, Surem, Peledysh Pairem and more), dates for 2026–2027 and how they are calculated.
• Prayers — dates of common and village prayers from published announcements.
• Rites (Yula) — the order of the traditional Mari prayer and family rites, quoted from Mari-language books with Russian glosses.
• Folk omens — weather, harvest and household omens and beliefs.
• Search — find holidays, omens and names with filters.
• Reminders — a morning "what's today" summary plus reminders 0–7 days ahead.
• Beautiful share cards for messengers.
• "Suggest a holiday / Report an error" form that works offline and sends later.
• Light and dark themes; Mari names of months and weekdays.

All data is built into the app and works offline. Every entry cites its source: Mari El Radio, the S. G. Chavain National Library, kelshymash.ru, Merkanash, the "Mari El" newspaper, and books by N. S. Popov & A. I. Tanygin ("Yumyn yula"), V. M. Mamaev and Yu. A. Kaliev.

Free, with unobtrusive ads.
